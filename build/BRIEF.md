# BRIEF — hale-digital scroll rebuild
Self-authored, not interviewed (autonomous run).

## The eight answers (brand voice)
1. Vibe: "Honest workshop. Warm paper. Quiet confidence." References: a well-lit hardware store catalogue; Wise.com warmth; a handwritten invoice done properly.
2. Scroll journey (their words): "They arrive doubting. First they see their own listing's problems. Then what it costs them. Then the fix, shown plainly. Then proof it holds. Then the two prices. Last: send one message."
3. Energy curve: calm open, sharper tension mid-page, one confident peak at the before/after reveal, quiet resolve at the end.
4. Feelings stage-by-stage: seen → stung → relieved → convinced → ready. THE ONE MOMENT: the dirty-listing/bright-listing wipe — the page itself 'fixes' a listing as you scroll.
5. One thing no site does: scrolling literally repairs a Google listing in front of you (the signature move).
6. Distance from premium-minimal: editorial-premium, restrained. Not brutalist, not maximalist.
7. Unbroken world or scenes: distinct scenes (chapters of one working day).
8. Assets owned: none — fully generated stills, no faces, SA context, no video budget.

## Feeling curve
- Act 1 Recognition: calm recognition ("that's my listing")
- Act 2 Tension: sting (the cost, named plainly)
- Act 3 Turn: relief turning to curiosity (THE PEAK — the repair wipe, largest span)
- Act 4 Substance: grounded trust (real process, no magic)
- Act 5 Range: simple choosing (two prices, asymmetric weight)
- Act 6 Commitment: quiet resolve (one action, held)

## Peak
The repair wipe (Act 3). Sentence a visitor would say: "It fixed the listing as I scrolled."

## Tell-someone sentence
"It's the site where scrolling fixes a plumber's Google listing right in front of you."

## Constraints
Free tools only: generated stills + CSS motion. Zero scrub video. Engine: scrollcraft.js/css,
tokens overridden to warm-paper palette (#ffffff/#f6f5f4 canvas, rgba(0,0,0,.95) ink,
#0075de accent, Source Serif 4 display / Source Sans 3 text). Hard rules from SKILL.md
apply in full: no em dashes visible, no scroll cues, no act counters, no eyebrows on every
section, varied anchors, no gradient text/glow, transform+opacity only, real stats only
(no invented counters), close resolves and holds.
